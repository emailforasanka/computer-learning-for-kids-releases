Privacy Policy

Computer Learning for Kids (Windows and Android)
Published by PulseCode Technology Solutions · Last updated: 27 October 2026

In short: the app does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information. Everything a child does in the app stays on the device it was played on.

Information stored on the device

To work, the app keeps the following only on your device:

  • Each player's first name (or nickname), chosen character and level
  • Game progress: stars, badges, stickers and daily-challenge streaks
  • Minutes played each day (for the parents' area and time limits), kept for 30 days
  • Pictures drawn in Paint Studio
  • Sound and display settings

This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, sign-in or email addresses
  • No advertising and no advertising IDs
  • No analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services
  • No chat or contact with other people
  • No in-app purchases
  • No use of the camera, microphone, contacts or location

Internet use

The games work fully offline. The Windows version checks for app updates on GitHub (github.com), which, like any website, can see the computer's IP address; no personal information is sent. The Android version is updated through Google Play.

Spoken instructions

Byte's voice uses the text-to-speech built into the device's operating system.

Sharing certificates

A parent can choose to save or share a certificate picture. This only happens when someone presses the Save/Share button, and the parent chooses where it goes.

Deleting information

A parent can delete a player (with their progress and pictures) in the Parents area. Uninstalling the app removes all of its information from the device.

Children's privacy

The app is designed for children aged 4–12 and follows the Google Play Families Policy. Because no personal information is collected, there is no information to access, correct or delete on our side.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
